Build Your Career with Us

Have fun. Cultivate your passion. Shape your future.

Working with us means thriving in a welcoming environment where your well-being matters as much as the joy of doing great work. Join a team that moves forward together—for you and with you.

Open positions

Dishwasher

You clean and organize dishes and kitchen equipment to keep the dishwashing area clean, organized, and running efficiently throughout service. Salary: Starting at $20/hour Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: No experience required

Floor Manager

You oversee dining room operations, lead the team, and ensure smooth, professional service. Schedule: Full-time Experience: Minimum 5 years in the restaurant industry, including at least 1 year in management

Pizzaman / Pizzaiolo

You prepare, top and bake pizzas according to the restaurant’s recipes and standards. Salary: Starting at $20/hour + tip sharing Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: Pizza experience is an asset

Delivery Driver

You deliver orders quickly and courteously throughout Lévis. Salary: Starting at $13.30/hour + individual tips Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: No experience required Requirements: Valid driver’s licence · Good knowledge of the Lévis area

Receptionist

You welcome guests in person and over the phone, manage reservations, and handle takeout and delivery orders while ensuring smooth communication with the team. Salary: $17/hour + tips Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: Comfortable with customer service and computer systems

Busser

You support the service team by clearing and resetting tables, keeping the dining room clean, and ensuring service stations stay stocked so service remains fast and smooth. Salary: Starting at $13.30/hour + tip sharing Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: No experience required

Bartender

You prepare and serve cocktails, wine and spirits quickly and accurately, while providing attentive service and contributing to the atmosphere of the bar. Salary: Starting at $13.30/hour + individual tips Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: Minimum 2 years of experience behind a bar or in table service

Server

You welcome and advise guests, take orders, and provide fast, attentive and professional service from the beginning to the end of the meal. Salary: Starting at $13.30/hour + individual tips Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: Minimum 2 years of table service experience

Line Cook

You prepare and assemble menu items consistently and efficiently. Salary: Starting at $24/hour + tip sharing Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: Minimum 3 years of kitchen experience or a completed DEP in Professional Cooking

Hostess

You welcome guests upon arrival, escort them to their table, distribute menus, and help the team keep the entrance and dining room clean, organized and welcoming. Salary: Starting at $17/hour + tip sharing Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: No experience required

Food Runner

You act as the link between the kitchen and dining room by quickly delivering dishes to the correct tables. Salary: Starting at $13.30/hour + tip sharing Schedule: Full-time or part-time Experience: No experience required
